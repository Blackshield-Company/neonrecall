# Night Shift Operations

The studio runs from 6 PM until 4 AM. This page covers the procedures for the night crew.

## Opening checklist

Turn on the street-front neon signs at sunset. Check the logbook for open issues from the previous shift. Power up the studio in the documented order and run the clock sync test on the drum machines before any session.

## Booking and sessions

Sessions are booked in four-hour blocks through the front desk. A late cancellation, less than 24 hours before the session, is charged at half the session rate. Overtime is billed in thirty-minute increments.

## Closing checklist

Back up the day's session files to the archive server and confirm that the backup job reported success. Power down studio monitors first. Switch off the neon signs and lock the vault.

## Incidents

Log every equipment fault in the logbook with the time, the device and the symptom. For anything involving smoke, a burning smell or exposed high voltage, cut the power at the breaker and call the on-call technician.
